1. **Fork-to-Star Ratio**: GitHub's fraud detection penalizes repositories with thousands of stars and near-zero forks. Maintain a healthy ratio of **$> 1:8$ (1 fork per 8 stars)**. 3. **Geographic Diversity**: Sudden bursts of stars from a single geographic IP range trigger automated spam flags. Traffic should be distributed across US East, US West, Europe, and APAC.
