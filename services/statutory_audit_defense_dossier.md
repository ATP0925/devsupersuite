## 3. Revenue Recognition Policy (ASC 606 & IFRS 15)

DevSuperSuite strictly applies the five-step revenue recognition framework across all commercial streams:

### 3.1 Five-Step Framework Execution
1. **Identify the Contract**: Bilaterally executed Master Services Agreement (MSA), Statement of Work (SOW), Retainer Pod Agreement, or Clickwrap Developer License Agreement.
2. **Identify Performance Obligations**:
   - *Stream A (Software Licenses)*: Delivery of standalone software package and unique Ed25519 cryptographic license key.
   - *Stream B (Turnkey Implementations)*: Milestone Deliverables defined in SOW (e.g. Milestone 1 Architecture Review & Core Context Slicing Integration).
   - *Stream C (Retainer Pods)*: Continuous availability of dedicated solutions engineering pod and AST compiler invariant maintenance over calendar month.
3. **Determine Transaction Price**: Stated invoice contract price net of statutory discounts (e.g. $24,500.00 USD for Milestone 1; $8,500.00/mo for Retainer Pods).
4. **Allocate Transaction Price**: Prices are discrete per SOW deliverable with zero cross-deliverable bundling ambiguity.
5. **Recognize Revenue**:
   - *Stream A*: Recognized point-in-time upon electronic delivery of license key and SHA-256 verified binary payload.
   - *Stream B*: Recognized upon formal bilateral execution of the Milestone Acceptance Certificate (`DSS-MAC-2026-001`).
   - *Stream C*: Recognized ratably over the 30-day service period as engineering services are rendered.

1. **Transaction Balance Invariant**:

   $$\sum_{\text{All Accounts}} \text{Ending Debits} \equiv \sum_{\text{All Accounts}} \text{Ending Credits}$$

   $$\text{Total Assets} - (\text{Total Liabilities} + \text{Shareholders' Equity} + \text{Current Year Net Income}) = 0.00$$
