## 🎯 Executive Summary & Transfer Pricing Policy Declaration

This dossier establishes the official transfer pricing policy and statutory documentation for **DevSuperSuite Tech Services** and its multinational affiliate entities in accordance with **OECD BEPS Action 13 (Country-by-Country Reporting, Master File, and Local File)**, **U.S. Internal Revenue Code §482**, and international tax treaties.

### Transfer Pricing Policy Core Tenets:
1. **Arm's Length Principle**: All intercompany transactions between DevSuperSuite entities are conducted strictly at arm's length, reflecting market conditions and economic returns that independent enterprises would realize under comparable circumstances.
2. **DEMPE Intangibles Governance**: Intellectual Property (IP) ownership and economic entitlement to intangible returns are allocated strictly based on the execution and control of **DEMPE functions** (Development, Enhancement, Maintenance, Protection, and Exploitation) pursuant to **OECD Chapter VI**.
3. **Routine Engineering Service Pricing (Cost Plus Method)**: Core contract software engineering, solutions integration, and customer support services performed by offshore operating delivery centers are remunerated under the **Cost Plus Method (CPM)** with a certified arm's length markup of **10.0%** (benchmark range: **8.0% – 12.0%**) on loaded operating costs.
4. **Routine Distribution Returns (Transactional Net Margin Method - TNMM)**: Regional software marketing, client acquisition, and distribution operations are compensated under the **Transactional Net Margin Method (TNMM)** targeting an operating margin (Operating Profit / Total Revenue) of **4.5%** (interquartile range: **3.0% – 6.0%**).
5. **Zero Artificial Profit Shifting**: DevSuperSuite maintains zero operations in uncooperative tax havens or non-substantive conduit structures. All profits align directly with economic substance, headcount, capital deployed, and operational value creation.

## 4. Economic Analysis & Transfer Pricing Method Selection

### Method Selection Hierarchy:
- **Comparable Uncontrolled Price (CUP) Method**: Rejected due to the proprietary and non-commodity nature of the specialized compiler AST algorithms.
- **Resale Price Method (RPM)**: Applicable to routine distributors with minimal value-add; TNMM preferred due to service and bundled software elements.
- **Cost Plus Method (CPM)**: **SELECTED** as the most appropriate method for intercompany software engineering, solutions delivery, and technical support services.
- **Transactional Net Margin Method (TNMM)**: **SELECTED** as the secondary/primary method for validating distributor operating returns and overall value chain allocation.
- **Profit Split Method (PSM)**: Evaluated and rejected as only one party (Principal) owns unique and valuable intangibles and makes non-routine contributions.

### Comparable Search & Arm's Length Interquartile Benchmarks:

To validate the arm's length nature of the service markup and distributor margin, DevSuperSuite Treasury conducted an empirical benchmarking study across independent comparable software service providers and distributors in relevant geographic markets:

- **DevSuperSuite Contract Policy Markup**: **10.00%** *(Directly aligned with market median)*

## 5. Intercompany Agreement Framework & Standard Clauses

All cross-border transactions are formalized in written legal agreements executed prior to the performance of services:

1. **Master Software R&D and Engineering Services Agreement**:
   - **Service Fee Formula**: $\text{Intercompany Invoice} = \text{Total Allowable Direct and Indirect Operating Costs} \times (1 + 0.10)$.
   - **Allowable Costs**: Engineering salaries, contractor fees, cloud compute allocated to R&D, office facility expenses, depreciation on developer hardware, and administrative overhead.
   - **Pass-Through Expenses**: Out-of-pocket cloud server hosting and third-party foundation token APIs are passed through at net cost with zero markup.
   - **Payment Terms**: Net 30 days via SWIFT bank wire in USD.
2. **Master Software IP License & Distribution Agreement**:
   - DevSuperSuite US LLC is granted a non-exclusive license to market, distribute, and sublicense the DevSuperSuite platform to enterprise clients across North America.
   - US LLC retains an arm's length distributor return of 4.5% Operating Margin; all residual revenue is remitted as an IP Royalty / Platform License Fee to DevSuperSuite Global Holdings.
