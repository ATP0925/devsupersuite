## 2. Master Billing Architecture & Service Models

DevSuperSuite provides three distinct engagement modalities engineered to serve different client maturity profiles while locking in predictable cash flow and healthy gross margins.

### 2.1 Time & Materials (T&M) Rate Card

Time & Materials contracts are deployed for rapid discovery, open-ended R&D, systems refactoring, context compressor integrations, and ad-hoc engineering sprints where scope cannot be rigidly bound.

#### 2.1.1 Standard Developer Hourly Rate Card

| Seniority Tier | Billable Rate (USD/hr) | Fully Loaded Direct Labor Cost (Max) | Target Gross Margin (%) | Minimum Experience / Technical Profile |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Junior Engineer / Specialist** | **$45.00** | $15.00 / hr | **66.7%** | 1–3 years experience; AST syntax parsing, test scaffolding, regression verification, documentation synthesis. |
| **Senior AI / Full-Stack Engineer** | **$75.00** | $25.00 / hr | **66.7%** | 4–7 years experience; OmniKernel AST compiler customization, AgentShield entropy engine integration, full-stack microservices, cloud scaling. |
| **Principal / Solutions Architect** | **$120.00** | $38.00 / hr | **68.3%** | 8+ years experience; Enterprise distributed architecture, custom MCP server protocols, invariant guard rule engines, security audits. |

#### 2.1.2 Timecard Logging & Billing Mechanics
1. **Tracking Granularity**: Time is logged in **15-minute (0.25-hour) increments**. Any fraction of an increment exceeding 5 minutes rounds up to the next 0.25-hour mark.
2. **Weekly Cutoff**: The operational week runs from Monday 00:00:00 UTC through Sunday 23:59:59 UTC.
3. **Weekly Billing Cycle**: T&M invoices are generated every Monday at 09:00 UTC covering the preceding week, accompanied by an audited breakdown of tasks, git commit hashes, and ticket references.
4. **Minimum Engagement**: All T&M engagements require a minimum initial deposit equal to **20 hours of blended project rate** held in escrow or credited against the first invoice.

#### 2.1.3 Rate Multipliers for Critical SLA Escalations
When a client requests priority emergency triage outside standard working hours:
- **Standard Hours (08:00 – 18:00 UTC, Mon–Fri)**: Base Rate Card (1.0x).
- **After-Hours / Urgent Weekend Sprints**: 1.5x Base Rate Card ($67.50 / $112.50 / $180.00 per hour).
- **Critical Production Emergency (P0 Outage / Security Breach)**: 2.0x Base Rate Card ($90.00 / $150.00 / $240.00 per hour, 4-hour minimum charge).

#### 2.2.2 Scope Governance & Change Order (CO) Protocol
Under no circumstances may engineering personnel implement requirements outside the initial Statement of Work (SOW) without a signed Commercial Change Order:
1. **Identification**: Any modification altering functional specifications, adding external integrations, or adding >8 engineering hours constitutes out-of-scope work.
2. **CO Pricing Structure**: All Change Orders are priced on a Fixed-Price basis or billed under T&M Rate Card plus a **15% project management surcharge**.
3. **Execution Rule**: Work on Change Orders will not begin until the client signs the CO and pays a minimum **50% mobilization deposit** for that change order.

#### 2.2.3 Deemed Acceptance Clause
To eliminate client delays during UAT:
- Clients have **7 calendar days** upon delivery of a milestone artifact to provide written, specific rejection notes referencing non-conformance with agreed specifications.
- If no written rejection is received within 7 calendar days, the milestone is contractually **Deemed Accepted**, and the corresponding invoice becomes immediately payable.

### 3.1 Payment Gateway Routing Architecture

| Gateway Channel | Primary Use Case | Target Transaction Size | Accepted Methods | Merchant Cost Structure | Surcharge Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stripe Invoicing** | SaaS retainers, corporate cards, T&M invoices under $5,000. | $500 – $5,000 USD | Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, Apple Pay, SEPA Direct Debit, Bacs. | 2.9% + $0.30 domestic; +1.5% cross-border; +1% FX conversion. | **3.5% Technology Convenience Fee** automatically added to credit card invoices, or baked into base quoting. |
| **PayPal Business** | International clients without corporate credit cards; self-serve checkout. | $100 – $2,500 USD | PayPal Wallet, Venmo, local bank debit, Pay in 4. | 3.49% + fixed fee ($0.49); +1.5% international cross-border fee. | Quoted with integrated card processing fees, or routed to bank direct. |

Clients contractually required to remit with wire instruction code `OUR` (sender pays all intermediary fees). |

### 3.2 Cross-Border Settlement Mechanics & Fee Optimization

#### 3.2.1 Surcharge and Processing Fee Pass-Through Policy
1. For all credit card settlements exceeding $1,000 USD, an explicit 3.2% **Commercial Processing Surcharge** is added to the invoice where legally permissible.
2. In jurisdictions prohibiting explicit credit card surcharges (e.g., specific EU member states), the contract price is adjusted to reflect the grossed-up value ($Invoice\_Gross = \frac{Net\_Target}{1 - 0.035}$).
3. Invoices above $5,000 USD default strictly to **SWIFT / Fedwire**. If an enterprise client insists on paying a >$5,000 invoice via credit card, they must provide written pre-authorization to absorb the 3.5% transaction cost.

#### 3.2.2 SWIFT Wire Instruction Codes (Mandatory Contract Language)
All international wire transfers must specify instruction code **OUR**:
- `OUR`: The remitter (client) pays all incoming, intermediary correspondent, and outgoing wire fees. DevSuperSuite must receive the exact net invoice balance in its beneficiary account.
- Invoices under-remitted due to intermediary bank deductions (e.g., $25 - $50 short) are not marked paid. The shortage is rolled into the next invoice as an unpaid balance.

### 3.3 Foreign Exchange (FX) Risk Mitigation & Treasury Policies

#### 3.3.1 Contract Currency Standard: United States Dollar (USD)
1. **Universal Peg**: All contracts, rate cards, retainers, and milestone agreements are strictly denominated and payable in **USD ($)**.
2. **Dual-Denominated Foreign Quotes**: If an enterprise client requires invoices denominated in EUR (€) or GBP (£), the conversion rate is pegged at the Bloomberg or Reuters mid-market rate at the time of contract execution plus a **3.5% FX volatility buffer**.

#### 3.3.2 Contractual FX Adjustment Clause
Every international contract permitting non-USD payment must incorporate the following mandatory treasury clause:
> *"Currency Fluctuation Adjustment: In the event the exchange rate between the invoice billing currency and the United States Dollar (USD) varies by more than three percent (±3.0%) between the date of invoice issuance and the date of settlement, DevSuperSuite Tech Services reserves the right to adjust the settlement amount or issue a balancing invoice reflecting the currency delta to ensure equivalent USD net realization."*

#### 3.3.3 Treasury Repatriation & Multi-Currency Holding Protocol
- DevSuperSuite maintains multi-currency collection accounts (USD, EUR, GBP) via global commercial banking partners.
- Funds collected in foreign currencies are retained in natural currency to pay localized expenses or converted to USD upon favorable trailing-stop exchange triggers (e.g., when market reaches 30-day moving average highs).
- Cross-currency balances exceeding $25,000 USD equivalent must be reviewed weekly by the Commercial Operations desk to execute automated spot conversions.

### 4.1 65%+ Gross Margin Target Economics & Unit Cost Modeling

Gross margin is calculated using standard financial accounting principles:
$$\text{Gross Margin } (\%) = \left( \frac{\text{Gross Realized Revenue} - \text{Total Direct Costs}}{\text{Gross Realized Revenue}} \right) \times 100$$

Where **Total Direct Costs** consists of:
1. **Fully Loaded Direct Labor (FDDL)**: Base engineer compensation, statutory taxes, benefits allocation, and performance bonuses.
2. **Direct Project Infrastructure**: Dedicated cloud staging servers, fine-tuning compute, vector database clusters, and specialized third-party LLM API inference tokens directly consumed by the project.

### 5.2 60-Day Accounts Receivable Dunning Lifecycle & Work Freeze Triggers

The following timeline dictates automated dunning actions, late interest compounding, and engineering work halts:

```
[Day -3]  Courtesy Upcoming Invoice Notification
[Day 0]   Invoice Due Date (Net-15 / Net-30 reached)
[Day +5]  Formal Overdue Reminder + Finance Desk Outreach
[Day +15] First Statutory Delinquency Notice (+1.5% Late Penalty Compounding)
[Day +30] SOFT FREEZE: Pull Requests frozen, non-critical sprint delivery paused
[Day +45] HARD FREEZE: Staging server teardown, repo access revoked, legal escalation
[Day +60] FORMAL DEFAULT: Commercial Collections Agency Assignment & Legal Demand
```

#### Detailed Stage Actions:

- **Day -3 (Pre-Due Reminder)**:
  - Automated transactional notification sent to client Accounts Payable email.
  - Includes PDF invoice attachment, direct 1-click Stripe payment link, and full SWIFT/Fedwire wire instructions.
- **Day 0 (Due Date Notice)**:
  - System reminder indicating invoice matures today.
- **Day +5 (First Overdue Alert)**:
  - Commercial Operations manager emails client primary billing contact.
  - Inquires whether remittance confirmation / wire transaction reference is available.
- **Day +15 (Formal Notice of Delinquency & Statutory Late Fee)**:
  - System applies a **1.5% monthly late financing charge** (18.0% APR) or the maximum allowable by law, calculated daily from the due date.
  - Formal letter issued by `billing@devsupersuite.com`.
  - Notice warns of impending service suspension at Day +30.
- **Day +30 (Soft Engineering Freeze)**:
  - **Engineering Sprint Freeze**: Developers are halted from merging new pull requests or initiating new feature branches.
  - Client Slack / Discord channels alerted of operational hold.
  - Critical production bugs continue to receive emergency patches for 7 days to preserve baseline goodwill.
- **Day +45 (Hard Freeze & Environment Deprovisioning)**:
  - Staging clusters, preview builds, and dedicated sandbox environments are automatically shut down.
  - Client collaborator access to GitHub/GitLab repositories is temporarily downgraded to read-only or suspended.
  - Notice of Immediate Contract Default issued.
- **Day +60 (Default Declaration & Legal Referral)**:
  - Contract formally terminated for material breach.
  - Account referred to DevSuperSuite external commercial collections counsel.
  - Client forfeit of intellectual property transfer rights for unremitted code deliverables (as governed by Section 7.2 of MSA).

### 5.3 Dispute Resolution & Partial Invoicing SLA

To prevent clients from withholding entire five-figure invoices due to minor item disagreements:
1. **5-Day Dispute Notification Window**: Clients must log any line-item billing dispute within **5 business days** of invoice receipt. Failure to log a dispute constitutes full acceptance of the charges.
2. **Mandatory Undisputed Portion Remittance**: If a client disputes a specific deliverable or timecard entry (e.g., $1,000 in dispute out of a $10,000 invoice), the client is legally required to **remit the undisputed $9,000 immediately** under the original credit terms.
