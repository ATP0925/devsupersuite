## 4. Rule of 40 & Magic Number Deep Dive

### 4.1 The Rule of 40 Benchmark
$$\text{Rule of 40} = \text{YoY Revenue Growth Rate \%} + \text{Free Cash Flow Margin \%}$$
- **Annual Revenue Growth Rate:** **65.0%** (Scaling from Year 1 to Year 3 pro forma).
- **Free Cash Flow Margin:** **32.2%** (Driven by upfront cash collection and low capital expenditures).
- **Consolidated Score:** **97.2%** (Top 1% of public and private enterprise software benchmarks).

### 4.2 SaaS Magic Number Benchmark
$$\text{Magic Number} = \frac{(\text{Quarterly Recognized Revenue}_{Q} - \text{Quarterly Recognized Revenue}_{Q-1}) \times 4}{\text{Sales \& Marketing Spend}_{Q-1}}$$
- **Quarterly Revenue Expansion:** $+420,000.00 \text{ USD}$
- **Annualized New Revenue:** $+1,680,000.00 \text{ USD}$
- **Prior Quarter Sales & Marketing Spend:** $\$908,000.00 \text{ USD}$
- **Calculated Magic Number:** **1.85x**
- *Interpretation*: Every dollar invested in customer acquisition and presales engineering returns **$1.85 USD in new annual recurring revenue**, verifying optimal conditions to accelerate growth capital deployment.
