## 2. United States Corporate Tax Operations (IRS IRC Chapters 3 & 4)

U.S. Internal Revenue Code (IRC) Chapter 3 (§§ 1441 and 1442) requires U.S. corporate withholding agents to deduct a **30.0% statutory federal tax** from gross payments made to foreign persons for U.S.-sourced income (Fixed, Determinable, Annual, or Periodical - FDAP income), unless a valid tax treaty exemption is documented prior to payment.

### 2.2 Treasury Regulation § 1.861-18 (Software Copyright vs Copyrighted Article)
For digital downloads of the DevSuperSuite Standalone CLI and Hacker/Agency packages:
- Under **U.S. Treasury Regulation § 1.861-18**, transactions in computer software are categorized into four distinct classes:
  1. Transfer of copyright rights (e.g., right to commercially reproduce or modify source code).
  2. Transfer of a **copyrighted article** (e.g., a copy of the standalone executable binary or license key).
  3. Provision of services relating to the software.
  4. Provision of "know-how".
- **Legal Determination:** DevSuperSuite software license keys grant access exclusively to a **copyrighted article** without granting the right to reproduce or commercially exploit the underlying source code. Under U.S. sourcing rules, sales of personal property by non-resident foreign entities are sourced outside the U.S., resulting in **0.0% U.S. source income and 0.0% withholding tax**.

## 3. European Union & United Kingdom VAT Compliance

### 3.1 EU VAT Directive 2006/112/EC Article 196 (B2B Reverse Charge)
For commercial transactions with business counterparties located within the European Union (Germany, France, Netherlands, Finland, etc.):
1. **Place of Supply (Article 44):** The place of supply for cross-border software engineering and enterprise consulting supplied to a taxable person (business) is the Member State where the customer has established their business.
2. **Reverse Charge Mechanism (Article 196):** When services are supplied by a supplier not established in the EU to an EU-established taxable person, VAT is payable by the recipient of the services under the **Reverse Charge Mechanism**.
3. **Mandatory Invoicing Legend:**
   > *"Zero-rated cross-border supply of digital software services. Subject to reverse charge pursuant to Article 196 of Council Directive 2006/112/EC. VAT to be accounted for by the customer at the applicable domestic rate."*
4. **VIES Verification:** The customer's VAT Identification Number is validated through the European Commission's VIES system prior to invoice dispatch.

### 3.2 EU One-Stop Shop (OSS) Framework for Retail B2C Consumers
For unaccredited retail developer consumers (B2C) purchasing through self-service merchant gateways (Gumroad, Stripe Checkout):
- When digital products are supplied to non-taxable persons (consumers) in the EU, VAT is due in the Member State of consumer consumption.
- Merchant of Record (MoR) gateways (e.g. Gumroad Inc. / Stripe Tax) collect and remit consumer destination VAT (e.g. Germany 19%, France 20%, Finland 25.5%) under the Non-Union OSS regime, shielding DevSuperSuite from local VAT registration burdens.

### 3.3 United Kingdom Post-Brexit B2B Rules
Under Section 55 of the UK Value Added Tax Act 1994 (VATA 1994):
- Cross-border software services supplied to UK VAT-registered businesses are zero-rated on the invoice.
- Invoicing text specifies: *"UK Reverse Charge applies pursuant to Section 55 VATA 1994. The customer is required to account for VAT on their UK VAT return."*

## 4. Indian & Cross-Border APAC Tax Protocol

### 4.1 Section 195 TDS & Equalisation Levy Mitigation
For Indian enterprise counterparties or international platforms routing through India:
1. **Section 195 Tax Deducted at Source (TDS):**
   - Statutory rate without treaty: 20.0% + applicable surcharge and health & education cess.
   - **DTAA Treaty Relief:** Under Article 7 (Business Profits) of the relevant bilateral DTAA, software engineering payments are taxable solely in the residence state absent an Indian PE.
   - **Compliance Documentation Dossier:**
     - Tax Residency Certificate (TRC) issued by the competent tax authority.
     - Electronically filed Form 10F (self-declaration of foreign tax residency).
     - Certified "No Permanent Establishment" (No PE) and "Beneficial Ownership" Declaration.
     - Result: **0.0% Indian TDS deduction**.
2. **Equalisation Levy 2.0% Exemption:**
   - The Finance Act 2024 abolished/neutralized the 2.0% Equalisation Levy on digital e-commerce supply, harmonizing cross-border technology transactions.

### 4.2 Goods & Services Tax (GST) Export of Services under LUT
Under Section 2(6) and Section 16 of the Integrated Goods and Services Tax (IGST) Act:
1. **Five Cumulative Export Criteria:**
   - Supplier is located in domestic jurisdiction.
   - Recipient is located outside domestic jurisdiction.
   - Place of supply is outside domestic jurisdiction.
   - Payment is received in convertible foreign exchange (USD, EUR, GBP).
   - Supplier and recipient are distinct legal persons.
2. **Letter of Undertaking (LUT: `DSS-LUT-FY2026-REG-0994`):**
   - Enables export of software and licensing without pre-payment of 18% IGST.
   - Zero-rated invoice annotation: *"SUPPLY MEANT FOR EXPORT UNDER BOND OR LETTER OF UNDERTAKING WITHOUT PAYMENT OF INTEGRATED TAX (LUT REF: DSS-LUT-FY2026-REG-0994)."*
3. **Electronic FIRA Reconciliation:**
   - Every inward wire remittance is mapped to an authorized dealer bank Foreign Inward Remittance Advice (FIRA) and Central Bank Purpose Code:
     - `P0802`: Software implementation services.
     - `P0807`: Off-site software supply & licensing.
