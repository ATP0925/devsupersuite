## 2. Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) & CAC Payback Mathematics

### A. Mathematical Formulation:
$$\text{LTV} = \frac{\text{Blended ARPU (Monthly)} \times \text{Gross Margin \%}}{\text{Monthly Logo Churn Rate}} \times \left(1 + \text{Net Expansion Multiplier}\right)$$

Alternatively, under empirical cohort observation:
$$\text{LTV} = \sum_{t=1}^{T} \text{Cohort ARR}_t \times \text{Gross Margin \%} \times \text{Discount Factor}_t$$

### B. DevSuperSuite Empirical Valuation Parameters:
- **Blended Monthly ARPU**: **$785.00 USD** (blended across developer licenses, team commercial packs, and enterprise retainer tiers).
- **Consolidated Gross Margin**: **79.30%** (direct compute, token APIs, and loaded solutions engineering labor deducted).
- **Average Customer Lifespan**: **20.0 Months** (contracted baseline cycle).
- **Monthly Gross Profit per Client**: **$622.50 USD** ($785.00 * 79.3%).
- **Gross-Margin Adjusted LTV**:
  $$\text{LTV} = \$622.50 \times 20.0 = \$12,450.00 \text{ USD}$$

### C. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) & Payback Analysis:
- **Direct Sales & Marketing Cost Base**:
  * Outbound enterprise pre-sales solutions engineering: $1,050.00 per enterprise logo.
  * Developer marketing, technical content & cloud sandbox compute: $430.00 per logo.
  * **Blended CAC**: **$1,480.00 USD**.
- **LTV / CAC Ratio**:
  $$\frac{\text{LTV}}{\text{CAC}} = \frac{\$12,450.00}{\$1,480.00} = \mathbf{8.41x \approx 8.4x}$$
- **CAC Payback Period**:
  $$\text{Payback Months} = \frac{\text{CAC}}{\text{Blended ARPU} \times \text{Gross Margin \%}} = \frac{\$1,480.00}{\$622.50} = \mathbf{2.38 \approx 2.4 \text{ Months}}$$
